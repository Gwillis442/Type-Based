Type-Based dictionary sources
Prepared by: Kevin Tieu

Grades 1-8
Starter words placed in the Texas grade bands.
These are not the official Texas required-vocabulary figures.
Texas adopted required lists for kindergarten-grade 8 in 2026
(19 TAC Chapter 110). Those figures can replace this starter set.

Grades 9-12
Academic Word List headwords, Averil Coxhead, 2000.
The list is published for teaching use by Victoria University of Wellington.
Sublists are split across grades 9, 10, 11, and 12.

Past 12th grade
Further Academic Word List headwords.
These are college and textbook words, not a newspaper list.

Meanings and sentences
Written for this game. They are short teaching lines, not copied
from a published dictionary.

Sound
None. The browser can speak a word later. No audio files are stored.
